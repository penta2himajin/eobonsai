# The decode GEMV headroom is now spent: 249.7 -> 293.7 GB/s effective DRAM

Derived from the two tracked ncu CSVs, pre- and post-`patches/0002`. This is analysis of existing
evidence, not a new profiling run, but the numbers were not computed anywhere before and they
change the answer to "is there any decode headroom left".

Method: the CSVs are ncu long format, one row per (launch, metric). Every `mul_mat_vec_q` launch
was grouped by grid, `dram__bytes.sum` and `gpu__time_duration.sum` summed per launch, and the
effective rate taken as total bytes over total time. Same weights are present in both runs, which
the per-shape byte counts confirm (48.5 MB for the 17408-row shape, 24.8 MB for 5120, 17.8 MB for
12288 in both).

      4 launches, 93.1 MB, 0.373 ms   ->  249.7 GB/s  =  90.8% of 275 GB/s
      shape      MB      ms     GB/s    %275
      17408    48.5   0.175    277.7   101.0%
      5120     24.8   0.103    241.8    87.9%
      12288    17.8   0.084    210.9    76.7%
      1024      1.9   0.011    175.7    63.9%

**+17.6% effective DRAM rate.** The shapes that were behind are the ones that moved: the 5120-row
shape went 87.9% -> 97.0% and the 12288-row shape 76.7% -> 98.6%, which is what removing the L1
re-read was supposed to do. The 17408-row shape was already at its ceiling and now reads above the
275 GB/s reference.

## What this means for the remaining headroom

The 8-13% GEMV gap in ADR 0006's correction was measured **before** `patches/0002` (the correction
is commit `cf07225`, the kernel change is the later `c441990`). After it, the GEMV reads at or above
every reference rate measured on this card: 97.9% of the 300 GB/s contiguous-buffer ceiling and
106.8% of the 275 GB/s geometry ceiling. `docs/gemv-l1-diagnosis.md` reaches the same conclusion by
a different route: no identified decode kernel target remains, and the residual is ordinary DRAM
efficiency.

Two caveats, both inherited from ADR 0006 and still true: ncu replays kernels and can hold clocks,
so a figure above the reference is not proof that the reference was beaten; and the reference itself
was measured in an ordinary CUDA run, not under ncu. The defensible statement is not "there is no
gap" but "there is no identified target", which is a weaker claim and the honest one.

## Consequences

- The only large lever left is tokens per weight pass, i.e. speculation (ADR 0003).
- Non-speculation decode has no identified mechanism for more than a few percent, and the
  non-GEMV part of the token is spread over about twenty kernel types with the largest at 2.8%.
- Draft-model speculation (`--spec-draft-model`, `draft-mtp` / `draft-eagle3` / `draft-dflash` /
  `draft-dspark`) is the one unrecovered multiple, and it waits on a draft model for this ternary
  packing rather than on code.
